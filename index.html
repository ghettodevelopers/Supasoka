Privacy Policy for FlexFit

Last Updated: January 28, 2026

FlexFit ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and health-related information. This Privacy Policy explains how FlexFit handles your data when you use our mobile application.

1. Overview

FlexFit is a health and fitness application designed to help users track physical activity, workouts, and healthy habits such as water intake. We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Important: All health and fitness information provided by FlexFit is for educational purposes only and should not replace professional medical advice.

2. Information We Collect

FlexFit may collect and store the following information locally on your device only:

  • Step count and physical activity data
  • Workout and exercise logs
  • Water intake tracking
  • Basic fitness progress information

We do not collect sensitive medical information such as heart rate, blood pressure, glucose levels, or medical history.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your data is used solely to provide app functionality, including:

  • Displaying your fitness and activity progress
  • Helping you track workouts and healthy habits
  • Providing general fitness and wellness guidance

We do not use your health data for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

All health and fitness data is stored locally on your device using secure device storage. FlexFit does not transmit your health data to any external servers.

Your data is protected by your device’s built-in security features (such as device encryption, screen lock, and operating system protections).

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your health or personal data with third parties.

6. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete your health and fitness data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the FlexFit app from your device, or
  • Clearing the app data in your device settings

7. Children’s Privacy

FlexFit is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Medical Disclaimer

FlexFit provides general health and wellness information including fitness exercises, step counting, activity tracking, and healthy eating guidance.

All content is for educational purposes only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

FlexFit is not a medical device and should not be used to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical advice and before starting any fitness program.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
